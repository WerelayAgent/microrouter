The direct rail

USDC in, inference out.

USDC on Robinhood Chain, sent straight to a deposit address from a wallet or an exchange withdrawal. No connection, signing, or account; 0% deposit fee.

On screen before you open a wallet

  • ShownAmount
    AsThe exact figure
    BecauseNo surprise line items appear after you send
  • ShownMint
    AsEPjFW… + verify link
    BecauseCheck the USDC mint yourself before trusting the QR
  • ShownNetwork
    AsRobinhood Chain, stated plainly
    BecauseWrong-chain sends are the #2 support class; we name the chain
  • ShownMinimum
    As$0.50
    BecauseThe direct rail avoids processor flat fees

Any USDC arriving at the address is credited, and partial payments accumulate. If an exchange fee turns your $20 into $19.98, you get $19.98 of credits.

After you send

  • StateConfirmed
    MeaningSeen on chain — crediting in ~15s
  • StateFinalized
    MeaningCredited — the credits are on your key

The pending screen is a real URL; it survives a refresh, a closed tab and a phone lock.

“I sent it but don’t see it” — a button, not an email

  • VerdictNot found on chain
    You are toldThe signature doesn't exist yet — with what to check
  • VerdictWrong token
    You are toldWhich token arrived, and that we credit USDC only
  • VerdictWrong destination
    You are toldWhere it went — and the recovery policy
  • VerdictConfirming
    You are toldHow many confirmations in, and roughly how long
  • VerdictAlready credited
    You are toldWith a link to the ledger row

Paste your transaction signature on the pending screen for one of five specific verdicts. Wrong-token and wrong-destination policies are in /legal/refunds.

Read before you pay

ConstraintAt a glanceWhat it means
No withdrawalsCredits buy inference only.Credits buy inference. They are not a withdrawable balance — we are not a money transmitter and do not want to become one, which is what keeps this cheap and KYC-free.
Credits never expireYour balance stays until you spend it.Expiring credits is a dark pattern. Your balance sits there until you spend it.
Rail minimums$0.50 direct Robinhood Chain · $5 other crypto · $20 cards.Direct Robinhood Chain payments start at $0.50. Relay and other crypto rails start at $5; cards start at $20.
Save both secretsAPI key plus permanent dashboard link.Accountless setup creates an API key and a permanent dashboard link. The link can replace a lost or rotated key; lose both and there is no personal recovery.
