Why MicroRouter

Less ceremony around every request

MicroRouter is deliberately narrower: open-model inventory, direct funding, and inspectable routing without a user account.

    Crypto deposit fee

    0% here versus 5% there at review; cards are 3.5% here versus about 5.5% there.

    Identity model

    Setup returns a key and dashboard link. Lose both and there is no personal recovery.

  • 1,000 = $1

    Pegged credits

    Routing receipt
